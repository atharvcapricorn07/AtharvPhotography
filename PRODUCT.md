# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Purpose

The photography portfolio of Atharv Kale, a high school photographer with more than eight years behind the camera, at atharvphotography.studio. It shows the work and brings in shoots: portraits, cars, events and prints.

## Users

Mostly people on phones arriving from Instagram (@atharvphotography07), often within the in-app browser. They came to see more photos, and some want to book. A smaller group checks the awards.

## Brand

Photos first; the name should not be the loudest thing. Polished to an Apple level: clean, confident type, perfect spacing, smooth motion that never feels gimmicky. Calm after the intro, with a few photos rather than a wall of them.

## Anti-references

Template portfolios, SaaS card layouts, stat tiles, floating pill navigation over photos, motion for its own sake.

## Constraints

Static site on GitHub Pages: plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, no build step for pages, self-hosted assets. The photo pipeline (`node generate-photos.js`) and the Formspree contact form stay as they are. No scroll-jacking or scroll-driven intro. Fast, no layout shift, respects reduced motion, keyboard and screen-reader accessible. Never add claims or numbers that aren't in `data/`.
