The Person Behind The Lens

Hi, I’m Atharv Kale, the creator of Atharv Photography. I’m currently a high school sophomore with a deep-rooted passion for capturing stories through the lens.

Beyond the camera, I find balance in the strategy of chess and the energy of tennis and basketball—but my truest excitement comes from freezing a fleeting moment in time.

With over 8 years of experience across portraiture, nature, and urban landscapes, I focus on the intersection of light and emotion. My goal is to create imagery that feels both raw and unforgettable.

To me, photography is more than just a composition; it’s about capturing the silence between moments. I'm constantly experimenting and evolving my craft to share a unique perspective of the world with you.
