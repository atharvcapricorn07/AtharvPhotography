About

Hi, I’m Atharv. I’m a high school student, and I’ve been taking photos for more than eight years.

I find focus in chess and movement in tennis and basketball, but my favourite part of any day is the quiet wait for a single frame. Photography, for me, is a deliberate search for the extraordinary inside the ordinary.

My work runs from close portraits to wide landscapes, with a lot of cars and wildlife in between. What ties it together is light and texture: low sun on chrome, fog over a lake, a rabbit that held still just long enough.

I’m still growing, still pushing my perspective, and always happy to take on new shoots.
