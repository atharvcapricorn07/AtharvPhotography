About

Hi, I’m Atharv Kale, the person behind Atharv Photography. I’m a high school student who has spent more than eight years learning to tell stories with a camera.

I find focus in the strategy of chess and movement in tennis and basketball, but my favourite part of any day is the quiet wait for a single frame. Photography, for me, is a deliberate search for the extraordinary inside the ordinary.

My work runs from close portraits to wide landscapes, with a lot of cars and wildlife in between. What ties it together is light and texture: low sun on chrome, fog over a lake, the fur on a rabbit that held still just long enough.

I believe great photography is about capturing the silence between moments. I’m still growing, still pushing my perspective, and always happy to take on new shoots.
