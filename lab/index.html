Lab

Four experiments for the home page. Try each one on your phone and on a computer, then pick the one that makes you go “wait, what?”

Particles

The photo is about 20,000 glowing dots. They scatter from your finger and swirl into the next photo.

Liquid

Drag across the photo and it ripples like water. Photos melt into each other.

Fly-through

Scroll to fly forward through a 3D tunnel of floating photos.

Infinite wall

Every photo on an endless wall. Fling it any direction; it never runs out.
